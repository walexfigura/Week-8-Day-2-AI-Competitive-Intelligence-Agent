## What we know (as of 2026-11-10)
- SipWorks announced a "LockSeal" leak-proof lid for the Metro on 2026-10-20
  (`NEWS-SW-001`, company press release). We have not tested it ourselves.
- SipWorks' sale banners usually include an explicit end date.

## Why this competitor matters now
Our Commute tumbler's slide lid is splash-resistant, **not** leak-proof. A direct
commuter competitor marketing a leak-proof lid targets one of our known limitations.
