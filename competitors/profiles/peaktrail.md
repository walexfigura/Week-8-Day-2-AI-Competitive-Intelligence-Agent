# Competitor Profile: PeakTrail

> **Fictional brand.** All details are invented for the Northstar Supply teaching project.
> Website fixtures use the reserved domain `peaktrail.example`.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Competitor ID | `peaktrail` |
| Segment | Insulated bottles and outdoor drinkware |
| Watch priority | **High** — most direct competitor to our best seller |
| Price position | Historically priced at parity with Northstar on 32 oz bottles ($39) |
| Warranty | 2 years (per product page footer, fictional) |
| Channels | Own website plus outdoor retail partners |

## What we know (as of 2026-11-10)
- The Ridge uses a "TrailCap" twist-off lid with a carry loop.
- The Ridge page has a variant picker; the default selected variant can change, so
  snapshots must record which variant was captured.
- PeakTrail also runs a Canadian storefront priced in CAD.

## What we do not know
- PeakTrail's costs, margins, sales volumes, or pricing strategy.
- Whether any price change is a promotion, a test, or a permanent change unless the
  page says so.
