## Company
Small US direct-to-consumer brand selling durable everyday products, mostly drinkware.
Mid-market pricing: usually above SipWorks (mass market), below TrailForge (premium).
Positioning: "Gear that doesn't let you down" — only QA-verified claims are marketed.
Pricing decisions: Head of Ecommerce. Copy/marketing: Marketing Lead. The analyst only recommends.

(insulation, durability); frequent travelers; leak-resistance/durability buyers.

## Priorities
- **High:** ≥10% price change on a direct comparable; new features targeting our
  differentiators (leak resistance, insulation, warranty); recurring (3+ unique reviews)
  complaints that our verified features address.
- **Medium:** availability changes on comparables; positioning moving toward our
  segments; new launches for our audience; 5–10% price changes on direct comparables.
- **Low:** small or non-comparable changes, colors/photos, one-off reviews,
  competitor shipping/service complaints.
- **No alert:** formatting-only changes; promotions ending as stated.
- **Insufficient evidence:** variant/currency/storefront mismatch, partial pages,
  rumors, anything about motives.
