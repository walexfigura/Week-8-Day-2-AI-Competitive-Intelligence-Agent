# Competitor research workflow

1. **List what we know and don't know.** For each competitor: segment, price position,
   the products/variants that compete with the user's products, and open unknowns
   (costs, strategy, sales — usually unknowable from public pages).
2. **Map comparability.** For every competitor variant, name the user's product it
   competes with and rate comparability: *direct* (same size/use), *partial* (same size,
   different tier or features), *none* (watch for positioning only).
3. **Collect observations.** From supplied files (Mode A) or permitted tools (Mode B).
   Each observation needs: product, variant/size, storefront/currency, price, list or
   strikethrough price, promotion label and end date, availability, features, headline,
   marketing copy, observation timestamp, source URL/ID.
4. **Compare over time.** See `change-detection.md`.
5. **Read announcements by reliability.** Company press releases show what the company
   *said*; anonymous forums and rumors cannot support a conclusion.
6. **Check reviews.** See `sentiment-analysis.md`.
7. **Check the user's product facts** before recommending anything; only verified
   features are differentiators.

Ask good questions: "Is the 32 oz variant listed lower in the same currency, and is a
promotion shown?" Avoid motive questions you cannot answer: "Why are they undercutting us?"
