# Northstar Supply — Ideal Customer Segments

> Fictional segments for the Northstar Supply teaching project. Percentages are
> illustrative internal estimates, not market research.

## 1. Daily commuters (~35% of drinkware buyers)
- Drive, bike, or take transit to work; carry a bottle or tumbler in a bag.
- **Care about:** fitting a cup holder, not leaking into a work bag, keeping coffee hot
  through the morning, easy one-handed use.
- **Main products:** Commute Tumbler 20 oz, Trailhead 24 oz.
- **Watch for:** competitor lids marketed as "leak-proof" for bags; commuter-focused
  messaging from outdoor brands.

## 2. Outdoor enthusiasts (~30%)
- Hikers, campers, paddlers. Buy larger bottles and value durability.
- **Care about:** insulation over long days, dent resistance, leak-free in a pack, weight.
- **Main products:** Summit 32 oz.
- **Watch for:** price moves on comparable 32 oz bottles; durability complaints.

## 3. Frequent travelers (~15%)
- Airport and road-trip users. Want one bottle that survives a suitcase.
- **Care about:** leak resistance, cleaning, a warranty they can rely on.

## 4. Leak-resistance and durability buyers (~20%, overlaps with all segments)
- Often previously owned a bottle that leaked. Read reviews carefully and compare lids.
- **Care about:** verified leak resistance, replaceable gaskets, long warranties.
- This is the segment our verified LeakLock lid serves best — and the segment most likely
  to be influenced by competitor reviews mentioning leaks.

## What our customers do *not* prioritize
- Lowest possible price (they accept paying a few dollars more for reliability).
- Novelty colors or limited editions (nice to have, rarely decisive).
