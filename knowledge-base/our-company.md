# Northstar Supply — Company Overview

> **Fictional company.** Northstar Supply is an invented business used throughout the
> *10 Weeks to AI Agents* series (Week 8, Day 1 built its customer support agent).
> Nothing here describes a real company.

## Who we are

Northstar Supply is a small direct-to-consumer ecommerce brand based in the United States.
We sell durable everyday products online through our own store and a small number of
retail partners. Our team is about 25 people, so we cannot react to every competitor move —
we need to know which changes actually matter.

## Product categories

| Category | Examples | Share of revenue (fictional) |
|---|---|---|
| Drinkware | Summit 32 oz bottle, Trailhead 24 oz bottle, Commute 20 oz tumbler | ~55% |
| Lighting | Lumen rechargeable desk lamp | ~20% |
| Home textiles | Cloudrest cotton throw | ~15% |
| Accessories | Replacement lids and gaskets | ~10% |

Competitive intelligence currently focuses on **drinkware**, our largest category.

## Business goals for the next two quarters

1. Protect the Summit 32 oz bottle's position as our best seller at **$39** without
   starting a price war.
2. Grow repeat purchases from daily commuters (Commute tumbler, Trailhead bottle).
3. Make **verified leak resistance** a clearer part of our drinkware positioning.
4. Decide by Q1 2027 whether the Commute tumbler needs a leak-proof lid option.

## General positioning

- **Promise:** "Gear that doesn't let you down" — durable products with honest claims.
- **Price position:** mid-market. We are usually priced at or slightly above mass-market
  brands (SipWorks) and below premium outdoor brands (TrailForge).
- **Proof over hype:** we only market features verified by our own QA testing. Marketing
  claims must be traceable to evidence listed in `our-products.md`.
- **Who decides what:** pricing changes are approved by the Head of Ecommerce; product-page
  and marketing copy changes by the Marketing Lead. The competitive intelligence agent
  only *recommends* investigations and actions — it never makes these changes.
